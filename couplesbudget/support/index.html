Support

We read everything that comes in.

Email support.growlight@gmail.com for anything: a bug, a question, a feature you wish existed, or a privacy request.

To help us sort it out faster, it’s useful to include your iOS version, the app version (Settings → About), and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Privacy requests

To get a copy of your data, use Settings → Export Data in the app — it runs straight away and gives you your transactions and income as CSV files.

To delete your account, use Settings → Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what that removes and what stays with your household. If you never signed in, there is no account and nothing of yours on our side — deleting the app removes the data.

For anything else — a correction, a question about your data — email the same address. We respond within 30 days, 45 for California residents.

A note on shared households

CouplesBudget shows both members of a household everything recorded in it. If you need something removed from a shared household’s history, get in touch and we’ll help.
