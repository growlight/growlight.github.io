CouplesBudget

Shared budgeting for two people. iOS.

CouplesBudget gives two people one shared view of their money. Every transaction either partner records is visible to the other — no separate books, no reconciling two apps at the end of the month. Budgets can be shared across the household or tracked per person.

It works offline and syncs when you're back on a connection, so recording a coffee takes a couple of seconds regardless of signal.
